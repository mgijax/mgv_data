Data for MGV have been obtained from the following resources:
  • Genome assemblies for 20 mouse strains from ENSEMBL release 116. All strain assemblies are "v3", except for M. caroli and M. pahari, both of which are v1.1, and C57BL/6J, which includes both GRCm39 and GRCm38.
  • Gene models for 19 mouse strains from ENSEMBL release 116.
  • Gene models for C57BL/6J from MGI. GRCm39 models are current with MGI; GRCm38 models are from 4 Jan 2021.
  • Genome assemblies for non-mouse genomes from Ensembl release 116: human (GRCh38), rat (mRatBN7.2), frog (X.tropicalis, UCB_Xtro_10.0), zebrafish (GRCz11), fly (BDGP6.54), worm (WBcel235), yeast (R64-1-1).
  • Genome assembly for X.laevis from NCBI (GCA_017654675.1_Xenopus_laevis_v10.1).
  • Gene models for non-mouse genomes are from the Alliance for Genome Resources release 9.0.0.
  • Orthology assertions are from the Alliance for Genome Resources release 9.0.0, strict orthology set.
